Why does my AI Visibility Score matter more than my Google ranking?

How is the AI Visibility Score different from a traditional SEO score?

Traditional SEO focuses on keyword rankings and backlinks. AI Visibility evaluates how clearly an LLM can parse your content, extract entities, and trust your information. You can rank #1 in Google and still score poorly with AI engines.
