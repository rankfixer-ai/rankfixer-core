The Talent Gap

There are 3.2 open positions for every 1 qualified AI candidate.

The global AI job market has roughly 1.6 million open positions but only ~518,000 qualified candidates — a 3.2:1 demand-to-supply ratio. A year ago it was 3.1:1. The gap is widening, not closing.

Tightest regions: Europe (1.9 candidates per 10 roles), Southeast Asia (1.4 per 10). North America is looser at 2.8 per 10, but still deeply under-supplied.

Average time-to-fill for AI roles hit 68 days in Q1 2026, up from 52 days a year earlier. Non-technical roles fill in 38 days.

What's Declining

Not every AI skill is growing. Some are being commoditized or absorbed:

  • Basic data science (pandas, sklearn, Jupyter): Still useful, but no longer sufficient for dedicated roles. Absorbed into general software engineering expectations.
  • Standalone NLP (spaCy, NLTK, custom transformers): Traditional NLP has been largely replaced by LLM API calls. Only 1% of postings mention NLP as a primary skill.
  • Prompt engineer as a job title: The skill stayed, the job dissolved into other roles. AI Engineer postings now mention RAG (13.6%) more than prompt engineering (8.9%).
  • Entry-level AI roles: Employment of 22-25 year olds in AI-exposed occupations trails the rest by 19%. Only 3% of ML Engineer postings are entry-level.

The AI Wage Premium

Workers with AI skills earn a 56% wage premium over colleagues in the same role without AI skills. A year earlier it was 25%. The premium is accelerating.

By role penetration: 37% of product manager postings require AI skills. 23.1% of designer roles. 19.1% of business analyst roles. 39.5% of principal-level postings.

Remote roles want it more: A remote posting is 3.2x more likely to require LLM experience than an on-site one.

The Five Role Clusters Driving Demand

1. Applied AI Engineer (RAG, Evals, Agents)

The most 2026-specific role. Ships LLM-powered products: retrieval pipelines, evaluation frameworks, multi-agent workflows. Median salary $176K.

2. Machine Learning Engineer

Builds, fine-tunes, and productionizes models. The operational backbone. Up 41.8% YoY. Median salary $185K.

3. AI Product Manager

Defines what AI features should do and ships them across engineering, legal, and commercial teams. Up 161% YoY. Median salary $172K.

4. Prompt / Context Engineer

The fastest-growing title (+194% YoY). Now evolving into "context engineering" — designing the full information environment for LLMs. Salary $95K – $155K.

5. AI Operations Specialist

Manages infrastructure, monitors performance, handles vendor relationships. Up 122% YoY. Still underhired relative to demand.
