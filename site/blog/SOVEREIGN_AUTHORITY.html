RankFixer is not a passive validator. It is a predictive forecasting engine that operates with full autonomy, governed by the RPES-compliant core. Our system does not wait for corruption to occur; it anticipates it.

Using proprietary zero-trust architecture, RankFixer models the trajectory of content credibility. It identifies entities, actions, and claims that are likely to become contested or falsified, allowing preemptive correction.

  1. LLM Governance & Oversight

We do not trust large language models blindly. Our RPES-aligned governance layer continuously audits AI outputs, cross-referencing them against a living schema of validated knowledge. This ensures that every AI-generated response adheres to the highest standards of factual fidelity.

When a discrepancy is detected, RankFixer does not merely flag it—it executes a remediation protocol. This includes: - Source Re-verification: Automatically re-checking citations against primary sources. - Confidence Recalibration: Adjusting the weight of information based on real-time model freshness. - Entity Salience Re-ranking: Ensuring that the most relevant and trustworthy entities dominate the narrative.

  1. Zero-Trust Verification

Every piece of data, every citation, every AI output is treated as hostile until proven benign. Our proprietary architecture ensures that no single point of failure can compromise the integrity of the system.
