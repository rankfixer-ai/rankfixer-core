    What is llms.txt?
    
      
    llms.txt is a proposed standard: a plain-text file at your domain root that tells AI assistants and crawlers what to read and link to. Think of it as robots.txt for LLMs.
    
      
    Only 72% of top SaaS domains publish one — from our open benchmark of 100 domains. Adding one is a low-effort way to stand out.
