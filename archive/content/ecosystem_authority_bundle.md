## Reddit Post (r/softwareengineering · r/MachineLearning)

**Title:** We built an SEO tool that literally evolves its own ranking strategies — here's how (and why we open-sourced the core)

**Body:**

Hey r/softwareengineering,

I'm a dev advocate for RankFixer, an open-source MIT GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) tool. We’re doing something a bit different: instead of hardcoding SEO rules, we treat ranking strategies as a population of “mutants” that evolve over time using a custom evolutionary framework called MEOS (https://github.com/rankfixer-ai/MEOS). Each generation, the tool mutates, crosses over, and selects the best-performing strategies based on real GEO metrics (visibility, engagement, etc.). The result? A 28.77% improvement in system fitness (baseline 0.749 → champion 0.9641) over our initial heuristic approach.

But we also learned the hard way that evolution without guardrails is chaos. That’s why RankFixer is governed by the RPES (Reasoned Policy Enforcement Standard) framework (https://github.com/rpes-foundation/rpes). RPES ensures that every mutation and policy change is transparent, auditable, and aligned with ethical SEO practices — no black-hat drift, no “survival of the sneakiest”. It’s a deterministic governance layer on top of stochastic optimization.

We’re sharing the core components (MEOS and RPES) as open-source because we believe the combination of evolutionary search + governance is the future of reliable AI tools. If you’ve ever wondered how to keep an ML system from going off the rails, or if you’re into metaheuristics, I’d love to hear your thoughts. AMA about the evolution loop, fitness functions, or RPES policy constraints!

Repo links: https://github.com/rankfixer-ai/MEOS and https://github.com/rpes-foundation/rpes

## Hacker News 'Show HN' Submission

**Title:** Show HN: RankFixer – SEO tool that evolved a 28.77% fitness gain using MEOS (open-source)

**Body:**

We built RankFixer, an MIT-licensed GEO tool that uses evolutionary optimization to improve search visibility. Instead of manual tuning, we run a mutation-selection loop on ranking strategies. Our MEOS framework (https://github.com/rankfixer-ai/MEOS) achieved a 28.77% improvement in system fitness (0.749 → 0.9641) on our internal benchmark.

To keep the evolution from going rogue, we wrapped it with RPES (https://github.com/rpes-foundation/rpes), a governance standard that enforces reasoned policies at every mutation step. Both MEOS and RPES are open-source and available for you to use in your own projects.
